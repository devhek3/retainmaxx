## Product Overview

RetainMaxx is a mobile-first application designed to help users retain useful information they discover through short-form content, particularly Instagram Reels, TikTok videos, and YouTube Shorts. During onboarding, users select at least five topics or domains they are interested in. Whenever they encounter short-form content they want to remember, they can share it directly with RetainMaxx. The app then extracts the essential information from that content, summarizes it into a concise and useful form, and automatically organizes it under the most relevant topic selected during onboarding. The MVP is intentionally simple: capture useful short-form content with minimal effort, transform it into retainable knowledge, and make that knowledge easy to find and revisit later.
